Information System Analysis and Design                                      Assignment 01



1 Assignment Overview
Assignment 01 introduces students to the first and most important stage of Informa-
tion System Analysis and Design: understanding the real-world problem before
designing the software system.

In this assignment, students investigate a realistic e-commerce business and analyze its:

 • business context;

 • stakeholders;

 • major business activities;

 • information;

 • current problems;

 • system scope;

 • system boundary;

 • preliminary information-system objectives.

The assignment deliberately does not require detailed UML modeling or implementation.
Students should first understand why an information system is needed and what business
problem it should address.

  Central question of Assignment 01

      What problem should the information system
                                         solve?
The work produced in this assignment will become the foundation for Assignment 02,
where students transform the identified problems and stakeholder needs into formal system
requirements.


2 Learning Objectives
After completing this assignment, students should be able to:

 1. Describe an organization’s business context and information-system needs.

 2. Identify and classify stakeholders involved in an e-commerce system.

3.2 Current Problems
The company currently experiences several information-management problems:

  necessary.

20 Academic Guidance

20.1 Analysis Before Design
Students should not begin by designing database tables or writing program code.

The recommended reasoning process is:



Understand the Business → Identify Problems → Identify Stakeholders → Understand Information → Define Scope


Only after this analysis should students proceed to requirements engineering.
